# Product image standard

The October 3, 2026 catalogue update follows the owner-selected local WordPress reference at `http://localhost:8001/products/`. All 30 products share square 1200 × 1200 WebP masters. Isolated products use a pale `#f6f6f4` matte and a centred subject whose longest side occupies approximately 80% of the canvas. Following the owner's refinement, measurement photographs, material close-ups and practical packing references fill the square edge to edge, with crops focused on readings, construction details and packing contents.

`src/standardized-product-images.json` maps original asset names to the corresponding masters under `public/images/products/standardized/`. Original assets remain available. Keep product identity, gallery ordering, media provenance and translations in the existing product data. The mapping is used by cards, galleries, body figures, social preview metadata and Product schema. Do not replace these callers individually.

The initial set contains 158 exports from the selected reference: 30 catalogue main images, 110 gallery background extractions and 18 preserved photographic references. Nine additional product-body images were edited with imagegen and exported to the same canvas, bringing the master count to 167. The reference originals were compared with the current website originals before import, including allowance for existing WebP compression and proportional delivery resizing. The nine new edits retain their original subjects and product counts; hands supporting the open foil pouch remain construction context.

The 18 photographic references now use geometric square crops of the original assets rather than a padded matte. Run `node scripts/prepare-reference-image-crops.mjs` to reproduce them, then rebuild to refresh responsive variants. The two thickness-measurement photographs use individual vertical offsets to retain the instrument readouts and contact points. This step crops and resizes the existing photographs without reconstructing their content.

The responsive build prepares 96, 384 and 768 pixel variants for these masters. Use `imageUrl`, `imageSize`, `productImageFile` and `responsiveImage` to retain consistent image addresses and intrinsic dimensions. Body figures, cards and galleries use the same square presentation without a second layer of padding or multiply blending.
